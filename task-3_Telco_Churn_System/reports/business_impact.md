# Business Impact Analysis - Telco Churn Prediction System

## 1. Objective
The objective of this analysis is to estimate the financial impact of reducing customer churn using the machine learning-based prediction system.

## 2. Business Problem
Customer churn directly affects telecom revenue due to loss of recurring monthly subscriptions. Retaining even a small percentage of customers can significantly increase profitability.

## 3. Assumptions Used
For business impact estimation, the following assumptions were made:

- Average revenue per customer per month: $50  
- Average customer lifetime: 12 months  
- Average lifetime value per customer: $600  
- Churn reduction achievable using ML system: 10%  
## 4. Revenue Impact Calculation

### Step 1: Estimated Customers Protected
Assume 1000 total customers:

- 10% churn reduction = 100 customers retained

### Step 2: Revenue per Retained Customer
- 50 × 12 = $600 per customer

### Step 3: Total Revenue Saved
- 100 × 600 = $60,000

## 5. Business Value of the System

The churn prediction system provides the following benefits:

- Early identification of high-risk customers  
- Targeted retention campaigns  
- Reduction in customer loss  
- Increased long-term revenue stability  
- Data-driven decision-making for marketing teams  

## 6. Strategic Impact

By integrating predictive analytics into customer management:

- Telecom companies can proactively intervene before churn occurs  
- Marketing resources are optimized for high-risk customers  
- Customer satisfaction and engagement improve  
## 7. Conclusion
The churn prediction system can potentially save up to **$60,000 per 1000 customers**, demonstrating strong financial and strategic value. This makes the system highly valuable for real-world telecom business operations.
